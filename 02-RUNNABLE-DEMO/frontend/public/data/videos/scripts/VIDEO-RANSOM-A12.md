Option C is unsafe because Uncoordinated rebooting may destroy volatile evidence and does not reliably stop propagation. Option D is unsafe because Deleting evidence is harmful, and payment is not a reliable recovery strategy or unilateral user decision. In practice: Activate the incident-response process, isolate affected systems as directed, protect clean backups, preserve evidence and involve system owners and authorities as appropriate.
