# Expert Challenge — Content and progression design

## Scope

Expert Challenge is a separate optional module with six organizational-level defensive decision scenarios. It is not merged into the canonical 45-question core bank. The module uses the same four-option assessment model and stores first attempts under the `Expert Challenge` level in local progress.

## Scenario coverage

1. **IR-E01 — Containment Under Uncertainty:** incident activation, proportionate isolation, evidence preservation and scoping.
2. **SUP-E02 — The Critical Supplier Component:** SBOM correlation, exposure, criticality, mitigation, patching and residual risk.
3. **INS-E03 — An Insider-Risk Signal:** objective evidence, privacy, HR/legal coordination and proportionate access controls.
4. **BEC-E04 — The Executive Payment Change:** independent out-of-band verification and payment-control discipline.
5. **GOV-E05 — The Board Risk Briefing:** verified facts, uncertainty, business impact and accountable update cadence.
6. **REC-E06 — Recovering After Ransomware:** trusted backups, clean recovery environment, identity remediation, validation and monitoring.

## Progression rule

The module unlocks after at least 10 Advanced first attempts with at least 75% first-attempt accuracy. The gate is transparent and deterministic; it is a practice recommendation only, not a validated measure of professional competence.

## Evidence and traceability

Each scenario contains a unique ID, four choices, one correct answer, four option-specific rationales, defensive action, key takeaway, reference IDs, explanation ID and video ID. The CSV mapping is `research/expert-challenge-traceability.csv`. Official references include NIST CSF 2.0 [17], NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 3 [28], NIST SP 800-218 [18], NIST SP 800-161 Rev. 1 Update 1 [37], CISA insider-threat guidance [32], NIST SP 800-53 [33], and the CISA/FBI/NSA #StopRansomware Guide [35].

## Media status

Six scripts, five-scene storyboards, generation prompts and WebVTT caption drafts are provided. No MP4 assets have been rendered or approved. All media entries remain `scripted_storyboard_caption_draft_media_not_generated`.

## Review requirements

An incident-response practitioner, risk/governance reviewer and instructional designer should review all six scenarios. Legal, privacy and regulatory obligations vary by organization and jurisdiction; scenarios intentionally direct learners to approved internal processes rather than attempting to state universal legal deadlines.
