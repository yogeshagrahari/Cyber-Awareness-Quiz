## Additional verification notes

- References [25]–[38] were added during the 12 November 2026 release-hardening review to resolve citations already used by the 45-question content. Official publication pages, PDFs or public advisories were checked where noted above.
- CISA’s official resource listing identifies the *Insider Threat Mitigation Guide (2026 Edition)* and links a 21.27 MB PDF ([38]). The resource-page metadata and 9 September 2026 release announcement are corroborated by indexed CISA page content; the linked full PDF was not downloaded or reviewed. [32] remains a separate 2024 fact sheet, not a substitute for the 2026 guide.
- NIST SP 800-53 Release 5.2.0 was announced on 27 August 2025; use the current NIST control catalogue, not an older static printout, for formal control mapping.

## Interpretation rules

1. Peer-reviewed empirical findings are cited only for the populations, interventions and outcomes the studies actually examined.
2. Review articles are not treated as proof that every included intervention is effective.
3. Immersive-VR evidence is classified as adjacent to browser-based WebGL.
4. AI-video findings in general education are classified as adjacent to cybersecurity awareness.
5. Vendor material is descriptive, not causal or independent efficacy evidence.
6. The 2025 phishing-training field study is an important caution but is not generalized to every training modality or institution.
7. The project-specific benefits of the proposed integration remain research hypotheses until evaluated.
