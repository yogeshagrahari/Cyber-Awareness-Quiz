Scenario: You use the same password for a shopping site and your school account. The shopping site reports a data breach. Safest response: Option C: Change the reused password to a unique one and review other accounts where it was reused. A breach at one service can expose a password reused elsewhere; unique credentials limit cross-service compromise. Option A is unsafe because Waiting leaves an exposed credential in use; address the reuse promptly. Option B is unsafe because MFA adds protection but does not make password reuse safe; stolen credentials can still be abused. Option D is unsafe because A predictable minor variation still preserves password reuse and may be guessable. In practice: Change the school password to a unique strong password, change other reused instances, and check account activity. Key takeaway: Use a unique password for every account so one service’s breach does not unlock another.
