Scenario: Two hosts are isolated after a suspected platform compromise, but data-access scope and recovery time remain unknown. Safest response: brief the board on verified facts, impacts, containment, uncertainties, owners and the next update. Option A is unsafe because No confirmed exfiltration does not prove that data was not accessed. Option B is unsafe because Leadership needs timely decision-ready information before attribution is complete. Option D is unsafe because Raw logs and attacker speculation can expose sensitive information and mislead leaders. In practice: separate facts, assumptions and unknowns, then record decisions and the update cadence. Key takeaway: communicate uncertainty clearly; do not promise what evidence cannot support.
