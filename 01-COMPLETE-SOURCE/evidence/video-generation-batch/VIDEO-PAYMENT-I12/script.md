Scenario: A regular supplier emails an updated invoice with new bank details and says payment must be made today to avoid a penalty. Safest response: Option D: Verify the change through a separately established phone number or approved vendor portal and follow dual-approval controls. Payment changes need independent verification through trusted records and the normal authorization process. Option A is unsafe because The email may provide an attacker-controlled number; use contact details already on file. Option C is unsafe because A test payment still sends funds to an unverified beneficiary and can create loss. In practice: Freeze the change until independently verified using contact information already on file, and apply the organization’s payment authorization controls.
