Scenario: An employee downloads an unusual volume of project files and connects a removable device before moving teams. Access is authorized, but context is incomplete. Safest response: preserve audit records, validate context and involve designated security, HR, privacy and legal stakeholders. Option A is unsafe because Public accusation and informal searches can violate privacy and compromise evidence. Option B is unsafe because Alerts alone do not establish malicious intent. Option C is unsafe because Authorized access does not make anomalous activity automatically benign. In practice: investigate objectively and apply proportionate, approved controls. Key takeaway: assess insider-risk indicators through evidence and due process.
