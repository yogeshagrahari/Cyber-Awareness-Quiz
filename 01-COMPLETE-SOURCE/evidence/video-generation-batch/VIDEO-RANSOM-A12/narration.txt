Scenario: A workstation displays a ransom note, and several shared files are becoming inaccessible. The user asks whether to reboot the machine and delete the note. Safest response: Option A: Isolate affected systems in a coordinated manner, activate the response plan and preserve evidence; follow recovery guidance from authorized responders. Containment is urgent, but should be coordinated; evidence and recovery decisions must be handled through incident response. Option B is unsafe because Connecting removable media to a potentially infected machine may expose backups to compromise. Option C is unsafe because Uncoordinated rebooting may destroy volatile evidence and does not reliably stop propagation. Option D is unsafe because Deleting evidence is harmful, and payment is not a reliable recovery strategy or unilateral user decision. In practice: Activate the incident-response process, isolate affected systems as directed, protect clean backups, preserve evidence and involve system owners and authorities as appropriate. Key takeaway: Contain ransomware safely and coordinate response; do not improvise recovery or destroy evidence.
