Scenario: Over ten minutes, your authenticator sends repeated sign-in approval prompts. You are not attempting to sign in, and a coworker says to approve one to stop them. Safest response: Option D: Deny the prompts, stop interacting with them and contact the security team immediately. Repeated uninitiated prompts can be authentication fatigue and may indicate a password is already known to an attacker. Option A is unsafe because Disabling MFA removes a protection and does not address the attempted sign-in. Option B is unsafe because Public disclosure may reveal account details and will not trigger formal incident response. Option C is unsafe because Approving may complete an attacker’s login; later action may be too late. In practice: Deny every uninitiated prompt and report immediately. Follow the identity team’s instructions for password reset, session revocation and authenticator review. Key takeaway: Repeated uninitiated prompts are a possible compromise signal, not a harmless notification problem.
