Scenario: During an investigation, a manager asks you to export all employee browsing history and personal messages, although the incident concerns access to one cloud folder. Safest response: Option C: Work with authorized investigators to identify relevant log sources, scope, retention and access controls; preserve integrity and document handling. Evidence collection should be authorized, relevant, minimized and handled with integrity. Option A is unsafe because Authorized, proportionate logs may be necessary for a legitimate investigation. Option B is unsafe because Altering original evidence destroys integrity and undermines investigative reliability. Option D is unsafe because Over-collection creates privacy, security and proportionality risks and may exceed the investigation’s authorization. In practice: Confirm authority and scope, preserve source records, restrict access, record collection metadata and involve privacy/legal roles where required. Key takeaway: Collect the minimum authorized evidence needed, preserve integrity and record chain of handling.
