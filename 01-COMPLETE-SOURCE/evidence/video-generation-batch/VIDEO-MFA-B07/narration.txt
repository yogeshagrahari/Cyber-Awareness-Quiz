Scenario: You are not signing in, but your phone displays an approval request for your work account. Safest response: Option B: Deny the request and report it to the helpdesk or security team. A prompt you did not initiate may indicate someone has your password or is attempting authentication. Option A is unsafe because Repeated prompts can be an authentication-fatigue tactic; approving a later one is unsafe. Option C is unsafe because Public sharing may expose account identifiers or organizational information and does not report the incident appropriately. Option D is unsafe because Approval may authorize an attacker’s login attempt. In practice: Deny the request, do not disclose any code, and report unexpected or repeated prompts through the organization’s support channel. Key takeaway: Only approve authentication you initiated and can confidently match to your sign-in.
