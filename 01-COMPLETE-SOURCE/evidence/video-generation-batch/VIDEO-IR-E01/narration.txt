Scenario: Alerts show file encryption, unusual service-account use and unusual outbound traffic. The initial access path is unknown. Safest response: activate the incident plan, isolate confirmed affected assets, preserve evidence and scope related hosts and accounts. Option B is unsafe because A blanket shutdown can destroy volatile evidence and disrupt unaffected services. Option C is unsafe because Waiting for attribution can allow active damage to continue. Option D is unsafe because Direct uncoordinated contact bypasses the authorized response structure. In practice: appoint an incident lead, preserve logs, contain proportionately and record decisions and unknowns. Key takeaway: respond to credible indicators without waiting for perfect attribution.
