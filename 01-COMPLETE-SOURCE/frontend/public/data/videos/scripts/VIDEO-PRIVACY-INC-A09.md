## Scenario

## Narration transcript
Scenario: A staff member emails a spreadsheet with personal identifiers to an external recipient with a similar name. The recipient has not yet confirmed deletion. Safest response: Option B: Notify the privacy/security incident channel promptly, preserve message details, request containment through approved procedures and let authorized roles assess notification obligations. Prompt escalation allows the organization to assess exposure, legal/contractual duties and necessary containment. Option A is unsafe because A deletion request may help but does not replace formal incident assessment, notification and evidence preservation. Option C is unsafe because Recall may fail or the recipient may already have accessed or copied the file. Option D is unsafe because Further disclosure increases exposure and is not an incident-response control. In practice: Report immediately, preserve relevant facts without unnecessary copying, request approved recipient-side containment and follow the privacy team’s incident process. Key takeaway: A misdirected data disclosure is an incident to assess even when accidental and even if recall is attempted.

## Correct response
B. Notify the privacy/security incident channel promptly, preserve message details, request containment through approved procedures and let authorized roles assess notification obligations.
